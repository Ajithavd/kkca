Journey from Darkness to Grace

The Pastor’s Testimony

“I will instruct you and teach you in the way you should go; I will guide you with My eye.” — Psalm 32:8

My name is Ps. P. A. Ravichandran. Raised in a family of five, my father served in the Indian Air Force and later worked at the State Bank of India. Though I had high aspirations for education and professional success, my life took a dark spiraling turn into emptiness and addiction, leading to severe despair and suicidal attempts.

In my darkest hour, God brought a divine encounter through Pastor M.K. Sam Sundaram with the words: “Young man, God has chosen you. He has a need for you.” After a life-threatening hospitalization where doctors gave up hope, the Lord Jesus appeared in a vision of bright light, saying: “I will give you life. You shall do My honorable ministry.”

1995 Baptism: Anointed with the Holy Spirit at Benny Hinn Marina Beach Crusade.
1999 Foundation: First church building laid in faith, consecrated July 27, 2000.
Revelation 19:16: Ministry named KING OF KINGS CHURCH — Lion of the Tribe of Judah.

26 Years of Overflowing Grace & Expansion

1999 – 2000

Foundation & Miracle Consecration

God miraculously altered government pension policies to fund the first church construction, consecrated on July 27, 2000.

2003 – 2007

Breakthrough & Family Blessing

God broke the curse of childlessness, blessing Ps. Ravichandran and Mrs. Jhansi with three beautiful daughters.

2010 – 2011

Official Name & Album Release

God revealed the name "KING OF KINGS CHURCH" (Rev 19:16). Released devotional album “Yesuvin Ratham” (The Blood of Jesus).

2013 – 2014

Faith Sanctuary Construction

Built a new 30x50 ft sanctuary entirely through faith—without taking loans or printing donation books.

2016 – Present

Sanctuary Portico & Multi-Hall Expansion

Expanded additional hall spaces and a church portico to accommodate our rapidly growing worshiping congregation.
